wrought like iron, spun like gold
the bond between us is a wire
carrying everything

we tremble out across it
sent like a message
to meet again

(yes we can
meet again)

it is thin as light
and many will ask
"on what do they walk?"

but it is no great mystery
(or it is the greatest mystery)
for it is the Light himself
on which we travel out to one another
aching to know the other
aching to be known

and what I'm sure of my love is that wire
which is twisted out of mystery
and resonant of heaven
is somehow carrying
everything
