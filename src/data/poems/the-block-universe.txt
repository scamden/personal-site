We were the children
We were startling light
We had open mouths and wide eyed stared into the stars

We are moving through
Moving on
Growing up
Learning the long lessons

But there we were young together
In the white green start of us

We sang like larks and lifted off
And swam the rays of sun together.

And everything is hurtling on,
Tearing through beauty and madness,
Through mundane days and sadness.
And our picture’s edges are blurring backward streaks of us in time.

And I can’t contain it all in my heart.
I can’t bear the bright hot pressure
of all that was
without
imagining that somehow it’s living still in You.

That somehow not one shining drop of this perfect everything falls into the dust.
But lives on
Beating heart red through your veins
always.
