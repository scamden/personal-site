awake! to hear the quiet beep at night.
subliminal, the sound is just below
the din of other hums but still with fright
i kick my legs from covers and with slow

stumpy movements of stiff joints i walk
out in to the minefield of the toys.
i squint my eyes and strain toward the squawk:
one pink walkie bravely issues the noise.

she’s shot and running low on her supplies,
sending SOS into the dark night.
only minutes more before the comm dies

but.. no my girl is sleeping tucked in tight.

i hold the power button down to save
may lines forever stay open i pray
