Washed with the scent of fresh commerce.
Heft the heavy glass door, across the secretive footbridge from the parking concrete.
The crisp echoey bathroom, the newest pop song is somehow nostalgic.
The four right chords, the brisk soap, common facets of memory. Significant, meaningful in emotion and somehow
empty as well.

Hokas in the land of cul-de-sacs
briefly crunch through the scent of dry pine. In this heat wave transports to camping in the Colorado mountains.
Pause to try to obey God but I’m too scared of getting dirty.
A hundred signs warn of dead ends.
Without making any turns I’m back where I started.
